Happy Birthday, Shruti

I don't say this enough, so let me put it in writing where you can't cut me off — I'm really lucky to have you as my sister.

You have this way of turning ordinary days into something worth remembering — whether that's convincing everyone to order "just one more" dish, turning a quick nap into a full afternoon, or already planning the next trip before this one's memories have even settled. Watching you enjoy life so fully, so unapologetically, is honestly one of my favorite things about you.

This year, I hope you eat everything that looks good, sleep in on every morning that matters, and go everywhere your heart's been quietly pulling you toward. I hope the people around you love you as loudly as you deserve. And wherever in the world you end up, I hope you always know there's someone back home who's endlessly proud of you.

Happy birthday, Shruti. Here's to another year of you being exactly, wonderfully you.

— Shagun 💙
